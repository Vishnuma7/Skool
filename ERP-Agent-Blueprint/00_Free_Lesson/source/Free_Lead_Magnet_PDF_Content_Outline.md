## Slide 4: Chatbot vs automation vs AI agent
- **Title:** What an AI agent really is
- **Chatbot:** answers questions, but can't see your data
- **Automation:** a recipe card, the same steps every time
- **AI agent:** a smart new intern who can read, think, use tools and act, with your sign-off
- **Suggested visual:** 3-column comparison: speech bubble / recipe card / intern with a badge.
