Sarah spends 12 hours a week copying ERP numbers, chasing suppliers and building the Monday report. That's 576 hours a year. In this lesson you'll meet Stocky, the AI agent we'll build together, and learn exactly what makes an agent different from a chatbot or a simple automation.

In this video, you'll see:

  • Stocky's Monday 7:00 AM email: 10 products checked, 4 below reorder point, and a risk warning on the pallet jack wheels
  • The Stocky workflow in n8n, and the human "Approve" click that creates draft orders in Odoo
  • The same question given to a chatbot, an automation and an AI agent
  • The five parts of every agent: brain, instructions, tools, memory and a human supervisor
  • The course map and your free toolkit

Key takeaways

  1. A chatbot talks, an automation follows, an agent reasons and acts.
  2. Every agent = brain + instructions + tools + memory + human.
  3. The goal is to give Sarah back her 576 hours, not to replace her.

Key terms

  • Chatbot: a brain that talks. No access to your systems; you do the work.
  • Automation: a recipe card. Same steps, same order, every time.
  • AI Agent: a smart new intern who reads, thinks, uses tools and acts toward a goal, with instructions and supervision.

  • Human in the loop: the manager's signature before the check goes out.

Lesson 1 · Activity: Agent or Not? + Wishlist
Add
File: Attach Video_01/V01_Activity_Agent_or_Not.xlsx.
Text

Time: 25-35 minutes · No technical skills needed. Works in Excel or Google Sheets.

Step by step

  1. Download the workbook and open the Agent_or_Not sheet.
  2. For each of the 20 BrightLeaf tasks, choose Chatbot, Automation, AI Agent or Not worth automating in the yellow column.
  3. Write a one-line reason for each. Ask yourself: is it the same every time? Does it need judgement? Does it need company data?
  4. Check your score at the top. Only then open the Answer_Key sheet.
  5. Read Sarah_Example to see how 12 hours a week becomes 576 hours a year.
  6. On My_Wishlist, list your own repetitive tasks. Fill in the yellow cells only; the grey cells calculate themselves.

You're done when: you have a score out of 20 and at least 5 tasks in your Wishlist. Keep the file: your #1 task is a great candidate for your capstone in Lesson 10.

Before Stocky can read an ERP, you need to understand one. In this lesson you'll set up your own free Odoo, learn the three words every ERP uses (module, record and field) and load BrightLeaf's 10 products, stock and reorder rules. It's your private test system, so you can't break anything.

In this video, you'll see:

  • What an ERP really is: the company's filing cabinet plus its rulebook

  • Creating product BL-1001 by hand, then importing 9 more in seconds

  1. An ERP is the central filing cabinet + rulebook.

  3. Your agent is only as good as your data.

Title
Lesson 2 · Slides & Notes
Add
File: Attach Video_02/V02_Slides.pdf.
Text

Download the slides to review the lesson at your own pace.

Key terms

  • ERP: the company's central filing cabinet + rulebook.
  • Module: a drawer in the cabinet (Inventory, Purchase, Sales, Contacts).
  • Record: one folder in a drawer (one product, one supplier, one purchase order). In Excel: a row.
  • Field: a labelled box on the form (Cost, Internal Reference). In Excel: a column.
  • SKU / Internal Reference: the product code, e.g. BL-1001. Never skip it.
  • Reorder rule: Min and Max. When stock drops below Min, refill up to Max.

Title
Lesson 2 · Activity: Build BrightLeaf in Your Odoo
Add
File 1: Attach Video_02/V02_Activity_Odoo_Setup_Workbook.xlsx. · File 2: Attach Video_02/V02_Activity_BrightLeaf_Product_Import.xlsx.
Text

Time: 30-40 minutes · Keep the workbook open next to Odoo while you work.

Step by step

  1. Download both files. Open the Setup Workbook and go to Setup_Checklist.
  2. Follow the 16 steps with Odoo open beside it. Mark each one Yes, No or Stuck.
  3. Write your Odoo URL and database name at the top of the checklist. You'll need them in Lesson 6.
  4. Import BL-1002 to BL-1010 from the Product Import file: Products → ⋮ menu → Import → Upload File → Test → Import. Don't rename its column headers.
  5. Enter the starting stock and reorder rules from Starting_Stock_Reorder. Leave the 4 low products low: they're Stocky's first job.
  6. Play Field_Detective (11 questions), then fill in Map_My_ERP for your own company.

You're done when: Inventory → Operations → Replenishment shows BL-1005 with "To Order 32", and your checklist reads 100%.
