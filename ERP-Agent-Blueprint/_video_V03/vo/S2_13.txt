The trick is to read them like columns that line up. The first date goes with the first rain number and the first wind number. So the 22nd: no rain, wind up to 16.8 kilometres an hour. But look at the 24th: 48.4 millimetres of rain. That's a seriously wet day, exactly the kind of number that should make Sarah call Global Lift Parts and ask whether her pallet jack wheels are still on schedule. And if the wind went above 60 or 70, you'd also want to ask whether the port is running.
